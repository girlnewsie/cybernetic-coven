Hekate’s most precious gift was split and stolen by upstart mortals. As heavens elevates those that wronged her as rulers of hell, the witch titan demands retribution — and only one thing will do. Hekate will break the Civilization Spell, even if that requires destroying the cosmos and starting anew.

Making enemies of gods and mortals alike, she wanders through the kingdoms of the dead. To her flock, the wispy dead and the rebel Plutus, she is the only hope for justice. For you, she has a single question:

Will you be the instrument of her vengeance?
